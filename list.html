Example: Steps to Register for Courses Online
  1. Create an Account
    1. Click on "Sign Up"
    2. Fill in your personal details
    3. Verify your email address
  2. Login to the System
    1. Enter your username
    2. Enter your password
  3. Register for Courses
    1. Browse available courses
    2. Add courses to your cart
      1. Select course
      2. Choose section
      3. Click "Add to Cart"
    3. Confirm and submit registration
  4. Pay Registration Fees
    1. Choose payment method
    2. Complete payment
  5. Print Confirmation Slip
